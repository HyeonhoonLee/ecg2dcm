# Example files

## synthetic_muse.xml / synthetic_muse.dcm

Generated by `make_synthetic_muse.py`. Every value is invented: the waveform is a sum
of Gaussian deflections at 72 beats per minute and the demographics are placeholders.
No part of it comes from a patient. Regenerate with:

    python make_synthetic_muse.py synthetic_muse.xml
    ecg2dcm-convert synthetic_muse.xml -o .

## guangxi_teaching_sinus_rhythm.xml / guangxi_teaching_sinus_rhythm.dcm

A GE MUSE 9.0.9 export published as a de-identified teaching example by the ECG
department of the First Affiliated Hospital of Guangxi Medical University, in the
repository [HexBladeNB/ECG-Master-Studio](https://github.com/HexBladeNB/ECG-Master-Studio)
(file `XML文件/窦性心律.XML`, "sinus rhythm"), under the MIT License reproduced below.
The file is included verbatim; patient name and ID are the publisher's placeholders,
and the acquisition timestamp, cart number and Chinese-language interpretation text
are as published. The `.dcm` next to it is the output of `ecg2dcm-convert` with the
default de-identification.
